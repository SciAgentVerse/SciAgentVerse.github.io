Organizers

An organizing team spanning agentic AI, information access, reinforcement learning, statistics, and trustworthy machine learning.

Organizing Chairs

Yuhan Liu

Postdoctoral Researcher, MBZUAI

Yuhan Liu is a Postdoctoral Researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). He received his Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, in 2025. His research focuses on trustworthy agentic AI for open information ecosystems, including LLM-agent-based social simulation of how misinformation spreads and evolves, multi-agent debate and verification for fact-checking, and the detection of AI-generated content, with publications at NeurIPS, WWW, IJCAI, SIGIR, EMNLP, and ACL. He has received the DAAD AInet Fellowship, the National Scholarship, and the ICML Gold Reviewer Award, and has delivered invited talks on misinformation and LLM agents at Microsoft Research Asia and Xiamen University. He serves as an Area Chair for ACL and EMNLP, served as a Session Chair of IJCAI 2024, and reviews for ICLR, ICML, NeurIPS, AAAI, ACM TOIS, and Nature Human Behaviour.

Haolun Wu

Postdoctoral Fellow, Stanford University & Mila

Haolun Wu is a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University and a researcher at Mila. His research focuses on trustworthy and adaptive AI, human feedback, personalization, and the evaluation and evolution of agentic information access systems. He has published over 40 papers in top venues and serves as a PC member in several top venues. He serves on the academic steering committee of vLLM Semantic Router and program committee of the Nexus for IntelligenCE (NICE) non-profit research community. He is a recipient of the Canada Governor General's Gold Medal in 2026. He is the leading organizer of the AAAI 2027 Workshop on Trustworthy Evolution of AI Agents: An Agent Operating System Perspective.

Wenyue Hua

Member of Technical Staff, Microsoft AI (MAI Superintelligence)

Wenyue Hua is a member of technical staff at MAI Superintelligence. She received her Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rutgers University and previously worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, Santa Barbara. Her research focuses on the trustworthiness and efficiency of LLM-based agents, multi-agent interaction, and language model reasoning. Her work explores efficient agent planning, trustworthy agent behavior, and the evaluation of reasoning capabilities. She has published at leading venues, including ICLR, NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, and TACL. She has served as a co-organizer of the ICLR 2026 Workshop on Memory for LLM-Based Agentic Systems and the 2nd COLM 2026 Workshop on Lifelong Agents: Learning, Aligning, and Evolving, and will serve as a co-organizer of the AAAI 2027 Workshop on Trustworthy Self-Evolving Agents. She has also served as an area chair for ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, EACL, and COLM, and as a reviewer for ICLR, NeurIPS, and ICML. She founded NICE, an AI/NLP research community that hosts talks on new papers and projects, to facilitate research exchange and invited presentations in AI.

Weinan Zhang

Professor, Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Weinan Zhang is currently a Professor at the School of Computer Science, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research areas include reinforcement learning, agentic AI, and physical AI. He has published over 200 research papers at prestigious international conferences and journals. He has been serving as an area chair at ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, KDD, etc. and an associate editor at TPAMI. Weinan earned his Ph.D. from the Computer Science Department of University College London and his B.E. from the ACM Honored Class of Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

Qiang Sun

Associate Professor, University of Toronto & MBZUAI

Qiang Sun is an Associate Professor in the Departments of Computer and Mathematical Sciences, Statistical Sciences, and Computer Science at the University of Toronto (UofT), and an affiliated professor at MBZUAI, where he leads the NeXAIS Lab. His research lies at the intersection of statistics and AI, with a focus on trustworthy AI, efficient generative AI (GenAI), and the foundations of AGI. Prior to joining UofT, he was an Associate Research Scholar at Princeton University. He received his Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC-CH) and B.S. in SCGY from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). In addition to his academic roles, Qiang serves as Action Editor for the Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR), Associate Editor for the Electronic Journal of Statistics (EJS), Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA), and Statistics and Computing, Area Chair for leading machine learning conferences, and a member of the Executive Committee of the BAAI Young Scientist Club. He is a recipient of the James E. Grizzle Distinguished Alumni Award from UNC-CH and the Connaught New Researcher Award, and has delivered a series of plenary talks.

Ling Pan

Assistant Professor, HKUST

Ling Pan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering and the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Her research focuses on theoretical understanding, algorithmic improvements and practical application of deep reinforcement learning and multiagent systems. Her work has been recognized by the AAAI New Faculty Highlights program and the Microsoft Research Ph.D. Fellowship (Asia). She has published several high-quality papers at top-tier ML and AI conferences and journals including ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR, and her works have been selected as oral/spotlight presentations multiple times. She also regularly serves as Area Chair in top AI and ML venues, e.g., ICML/NeurIPS/ICLR/KDD/RLC. She served as the journal track co-chair for ACML 2026, and co-organized the Safe Generative AI Workshop (NeurIPS 2024), Agent-based Information Retrieval Workshop (SIGIR 2024 and 2025), and Mila GFlowNet Workshop 2023.

Jian Kang

Assistant Professor, MBZUAI

Jian Kang is an Assistant Professor in the Division of Computing and Mathematical Sciences at MBZUAI. His research focuses on personalized AI systems to model and understand the interconnected world, including trustworthy generative AI, graph machine learning and their applications in recommendation and scientific knowledge discovery. He has received EMNLP 2025 SAC Highlights, Rising Stars in Data Science, and Mavis Future Faculty Fellowship. He is the associate editor for several journals including ACM Computing Surveys, Statistics and Computing, and Science Digitalization. He served in the organizing committee for multiple data mining and machine learning conferences, including LoG 2026, CIKM 2026, IEEE Big Data 2026, LoG 2025, CIKM 2025, and KDD 2024. He also organized the MBZUAI Workshop on Science of Graphs and Graph for Science, the special session on multimodal agentic systems at MMM 2027, as well as the 2nd Workshop on Secure and Trustworthy Large Language Models at KDD 2026 and the 1st and 2nd Workshops on Trustworthy Learning on Graphs at WWW 2024 and CIKM 2022.
